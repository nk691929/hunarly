# ADR 0002: International positioning

Status: accepted

Decision: Hunarly serves job seekers worldwide. Pakistan is a launch market with local payment
options and market-specific sections, not a limit on the product.

Consequences: Copy and metadata use international wording. Pricing supports USD, EUR, GBP and PKR.
Templates and sections support both resume and CV conventions. Locale routing (including Urdu RTL)
is scaffolded later and does not change the default English experience.
